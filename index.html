02 / Approach

From the first question
to the final detail.

Define. Design. Build. Open a discipline to look closer.

01Define.

Start with the problem. Clarify who the product is for, what they need to do, and what a useful first version should make possible.

Questions to resolve
Audience and context.
Core journeys and priorities.
A clear scope for the first release.
02Design.

Make the idea tangible. Explore structure, language, and interaction in a working prototype so that decisions can be experienced and discussed.

Details to work through
Information and navigation.
Visual direction and interaction.
Responsive layouts and accessible states.
03Build.

Carry the design into working software. Connect the essential pieces, test real journeys, and refine the details that shape everyday use.

Work toward delivery
Implementation and integration.
Keyboard, touch, and browser checks.
Release preparation and handoff.
